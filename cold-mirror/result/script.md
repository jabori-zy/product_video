# Cold Mirror · Promo Film Script

**Working title:** 《同一个她》 *One Face, Every Look*
**Length:** 36 s (18 bars). S12, the street walk, runs 3 bars; every other shot runs 1 bar.
**Format:** 1080×1920 vertical (9:16), 24 fps. Frame every shot for vertical.
**Music:** 120 BPM, so 1 beat = 0.5 s = 12 frames and 1 bar = 2 s = 48 frames. Every hair change lands on a beat.
**Production:** Higgsfield makes the final full-HD shots. Remotion makes the timing preview (animatic), using the stills in `../assets/` as stand-ins.

---

## 1. Core idea

The film is not about the UI. It is about one face with many hairstyles.

The film has a single visual device, and it comes from the real product. On the 换发色成功 (colour-change success) page, a **vertical split line** separates the "before" photo from the "after" photo. In the film, that line becomes a **sweep of light**. Every hair change happens when the line passes over her:

- **In the mirror:** the line is the product's own compare slider.
- **On the street:** a real vertical edge does the same job, such as a lamp post, a glass door edge, a passing bus or a phone flash.

Because the same line appears in both places, the product and the real world feel continuous. What the mirror previews, reality delivers.

There are two bridges between product and reality:
1. **Mirror → street** (end of Act 1): the camera goes *through* the mirror.
2. **Street → mirror** (end of Act 2): a shop-window reflection becomes the Cold Mirror again.

The film is a loop: barbershop → street → barbershop.

## 4. Sound

**Style:** Korean dance-pop / EDM at 120 BPM in A minor (Am–F–C–G). Bars 1–5 build up from a filtered intro; bar 6 is the build (snare roll, noise riser, the last half-beat silent); the drop lands on bar 7 as she steps through the mirror and carries through bar 14, the end of the street walk (four-on-the-floor kick, pumping supersaw, 808, vocal chops).

- **Bars 1–2:** room tone, a glass "ting" when the light runs down the edge, and a soft synth pad.
- **Bars 3–6:** a minimal pluck groove. Each mirror sweep gets a glassy *shhk* riser of about 6 frames, and each tap is a soft click. Bridge 1 gets a reverse-reverb swell into the door opening, and the sunlight hits on the downbeat with the full beat dropping in.
- **Bars 7–14:** the full track. The phone shutter is a percussion element: on the beat in bars 8–11 and on half-beats in bar 12. Add the whoosh of the passing bus.
- **Bar 14 → 15:** hard cut to one beat of silence, then street ambience and a single scissor *snip* on the bar-13 downbeat. The beat returns, half-time.
- **Bars 16–18:** a click on each studio light, chimes as the slogan halves land and as the words swap, then the final chord.
- Mix to −14 LUFS, true peak −2 dBTP (same as the other deliverables).

## 6. Production notes for Higgsfield

1. **Identity first.** `model.JPG` is the only photo of her. Turn it into a character sheet before anything else (front, ¾, profile, full body, same outfit), then train a Soul ID from that sheet. Every shot after that uses the Soul ID.
2. **Keyframes before motion.** For every look, make a still from the *same* base frame with image-edit, using the matching library image as the hair reference. Then animate with image-to-video using start and end frames. This is what keeps her face identical across looks.
3. **Hairstyle references must show her face.** Never pass a hairstyle-library photo (another model) straight to the video model, because the face drifts to that model. First make a still of *her* with each hairstyle (`higgsfield/looks.sh`, Seedream 5.0 Pro), then reference only those stills.
4. **Locked-composition shots (5, 11, 12) are stills, not video.** Generate one base selfie, then hair-edit it 12 times, and add parallax or a slight handheld drift in compositing. They will be faster to make and more consistent.
5. **Bridges are the hardest shots.** Generate Shot 6 as two clips:
   - (a) push-in on the mirror, ending on the glass plane;
   - (b) a street reveal that starts from a matching frame.
   Join them in the edit on the refraction ripple. Make Shot 13 the same way: (a) reflection rack-focus, (b) barbershop pull-back.
6. **Clip length ≤ 5 s.** No shot in this script is longer than 2 s on screen, so generate each with handles of at least 1 s on both sides.
7. **Device.** The repo has no hardware photo. Generate the hero device (portrait mirror + base) once with the product-photoshoot skill, approve it, and use it as a fixed reference in Shots 1–6, 13 and 14.
8. **Remotion preview.** Built in `../demo/` → `demo/out/demo_v4.mp4` (vertical, 36 s; `demo_v1.mp4` is the landscape cut, `demo_v2`/`demo_v3` earlier vertical cuts). All timing is in `demo/src/timing.json` (24 fps, 120 BPM, one shot per bar); the picture, the music and the asset script all read it. Swap the placeholders for Higgsfield clips shot by shot.

## 7. Alternative concepts

**A. 《橱窗》 Shop windows (one take, 20 s).** She walks down one street in a single continuous take. Every shop window she passes reflects a different hairstyle while the real her stays in her original brown waves. At the end of the street stands a Cold Mirror. She looks into it, chooses, and the real her changes. *Strength:* very elegant and keeps the mirror motif throughout. *Risk:* the one-take shot is hard for AI, so plan it as stitched segments hidden behind pillars.

**B. 《360°》 Orbit (15 s, social).** A slow 360° orbit around her standing in front of the mirror. Each quarter turn shows a new look, and each change is hidden behind her own turning head. This connects to the app's real 3D 展示 (3D view) feature. *Strength:* simple, and works well in vertical 9:16.

**C. 《一天》 One day (30 s).** Her hair follows the time of day: morning commute (sleek straight hair, 冷咖棕) → lunch with friends (twin tails, 草莓红) → gallery afternoon (French curls, 奶芋紫灰) → night out (wolf cut, 雾霾蓝) → home, back to the mirror. Each scene ends on a mirror, phone or window, which cuts to the next. *Strength:* tells a story about "a look for every moment". *Risk:* needs more locations.

**D. 《她们》 Them (25 s).** Split-screen grid. It starts with one face and keeps doubling: 1 → 2 → 4 → 16 tiles, the same woman in 16 different looks, all blinking and smiling in sync. Then everything collapses back into one mirror reflection. *Strength:* the cheapest to generate (stills plus subtle motion) and very scroll-stopping.
