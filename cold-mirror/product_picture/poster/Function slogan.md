## Three features (bottom row)

The reference format is a line icon, a short title of 4–6 characters, then a subline of about 8 characters made of two halves split by a comma. Example: 「高保真音质」 / 「澎湃低音，细节毕现」.

### 1 · 4K HD Camera

- **Title:** **4K 高清摄像头**
- **Subline:** **发丝毕现，清晰呈现** ("every strand visible, rendered clearly")
- **Alternatives:**
  - 「清晰度，无可比拟」 (literal "unmatched clarity"; see the ad-law note below)
  - 「纤毫毕现，清晰如真」
- **Icon:** a camera lens, drawn as two concentric circles with a small highlight dot.

### 2 · Premium Mirror

- **Title:** **高级镜面**
- **Subline:** **质感高级，通透清晰** ("sophisticated feel, crystal clear")
- **Alternatives:**
  - 「精致通透，一镜清晰」
  - 「镜面通透，质感出众」
- **Icon:** a tall rounded rectangle (the mirror) with two short diagonal shine lines in its upper corner.

### 3 · Self-developed model

- **Title:** **自研模型**
- **Subline, line 1:** **真人还原，无 AI 感** ("true-to-life portrait, no AI look")
- **Subline, line 2:** **40 秒真实换发** ("a real hair change in 40 s")
- **Compact alternative** (one line, to keep all three columns the same height):
  - 「真人还原，40 秒换发」 (drops "no AI look")
  - 「无 AI 感，40 秒换发」 (drops "true-to-life portrait")
- **Icon:** a chip, drawn as a rounded square with short pins on all four sides and a single hair-strand curve inside. Don't use a sparkle or AI-style icon, which would contradict 「无 AI 感」.

Covering all four claims from the brief (accurate portrait, no AI look, real hair change, 40 s) takes a second subline in column 3. Columns align on their icons, so the extra line hangs below the other two. Apple pages do this too. The compact alternatives keep the row even but drop one claim.

## Wording checks

- **「无 AI 感」, not 「无 AI」.** The brief's "AI-free" can't be literal: the product runs a model. 「无 AI 感」 says what is meant (the result doesn't look AI-made) without claiming no AI is used.
- **Ad law (《广告法》 absolute terms).** 「无可比拟」 ("unmatched"), and words like 最, 第一 or 顶级, count as absolute claims and are risky in a Chinese advertisement. That's why the chosen line for feature 1 is the descriptive 「发丝毕现，清晰呈现」.
- **Disclaimer.** The film ends with 「呈现为AI效果，实际请与发型师沟通」. If the poster is used commercially, add the same line in small grey type at the very bottom. It sits outside the reference layout, so it is optional for a mood poster.
- **Spacing.** Put a space between Latin letters or digits and Chinese characters (「4K 高清摄像头」, 「40 秒」, 「无 AI 感」), as Apple's Chinese pages do.
